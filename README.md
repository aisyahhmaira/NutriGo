# NutriGo — AI-Powered Meal Planning & Fitness Tracking

**Project Type:** Health-tech startup concept & interactive UI/UX prototype  

## Project Overview

**NutriGo** is a proposed health-tech mobile application that brings AI-powered meal planning, fitness tracking, and nutrition-focused meal delivery into one experience. The concept is designed to help users make food choices that fit their health goals, dietary preferences, and daily activities.

Developed as part of an **Entrepreneurship in Computing** project, NutriGo combines a **Figma application prototype** with startup planning, including a business model, marketing strategy, and financial forecasting. The project focused on product design and business feasibility rather than building a production mobile app.

## Project Highlights

The following features represent the **proposed application concept and prototype**:

| Feature | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **AI-powered meal planning** | Personalized meal plan recommendations based on calorie requirements, fitness goals, dietary preferences, and allergies. |
| **Fitness & nutrition tracking** | A concept for monitoring nutrition alongside fitness activity, with proposed fitness-app synchronization to adapt recommendations. |
| **Meal delivery & QR tracking** | An integrated meal delivery concept with QR-code access to calorie and ingredient information. |
| **Community forum** | A space for users to share experiences, interact, and exchange wellness-related ideas. |
| **Daily health tips** | Short educational content intended to support users' nutrition and fitness awareness. |

## Entrepreneurship & Business Planning

In addition to the prototype, our project explored the planning involved in developing a digital health startup:

- **Business model:** Defined how the proposed product could deliver value to its users.
- **Marketing strategy:** Planned approaches for positioning and promoting the NutriGo concept.
- **Financial forecasting:** Developed financial projections to support the proposed business.
- **Product pitching:** Prepared the concept and supporting materials for presentation.
